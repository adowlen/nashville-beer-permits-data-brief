# Sources - Nashville Beer Permits brief (2026-11-06)

All figures are drawn from the sources below, pulled on the dates stated. No figure in the brief comes from an unnamed source.

1. **Nashville Beer Permit Locations** - Beer_Permit_Locations_Feature_Layer_view, layer 0, ArcGIS FeatureServer hosted at services2.arcgis.com/HdTo6HJqh92wn4D8 (Metro Nashville open data / hubNashville). Full register pulled 2026-11-06: 2,117 records. Fields used: Permit__, Status, Permit_Type, Permit_Sub_Type, Permit_Subtype_Description, Date_Entered, Date_Issued, Parcel, Business_Name, Business_Owner, Address, City, State, ZIP, Council_Dist, Census_Tract, Lon, Lat. Live count on 2026-11-05 was 2,115; the register updates daily and the brief's snapshot is the 2026-11-06 pull (data/pull_log.json, data/beer_permits_raw.csv).
2. **Metro Nashville Police Department Incidents** - Metro_Nashville_Police_Department_Incidents_view, layer 0, same ArcGIS host. ZIP-grouped counts pulled 2026-11-06: 946,569 records served, 505,773 with a numeric ZIP code, 440,796 with no ZIP and 1 coded "UNK" (46.6 percent without a numeric ZIP). The published analysis of this layer is the Machine Readable Nashville Police Incidents brief (2026-11-05), https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-police-incidents-data-brief - its snapshot held 945,811 records; the served count grows daily, which is why this brief re-pulled the ZIP counts on 2026-11-06 and prints the snapshot it used.
3. **Denver liquor licenses comparison** - figures are taken from the published Machine Readable Denver Liquor Licenses brief (2026-10-24), https://github.com/adowlen/denver-liquor-licenses-data-brief, whose source is ODC_BUSN_LIQUORLICENSES_P (Denver Open Data, ArcGIS FeatureServer layer 27), pulled 2026-10-23: 1,965 active licenses, 1,498 on-premises (76.2 percent), largest neighborhood Five Points with 174 (8.9 percent). Both scopes are labeled wherever the comparison is printed: Denver covers all liquor license classes by neighborhood; Nashville here is beer permits only, by council district.

Derived figures (labeled derived wherever the computation is not a direct count): all shares and percentages (permits divided by permits in the same register and snapshot), median and percentile date gaps (Date_Issued minus Date_Entered), the Spearman rank correlation across the 33 ZIP codes present in both registers (0.904), the beer-to-police share ratios by ZIP, and the District 15 count with Gaylord-named permits removed (127).
